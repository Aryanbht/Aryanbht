### 💫 About Me

I'm a **second-year CS student from India**, building things long before I was "senior enough". I'm deeply passionate about the intersection of **Machine Learning, AI APIs, and Full-Stack Engineering**.

- 🏗️ **Founder of ShowUp** — A portfolio and credibility platform designed specifically for non-tier-1 college students.
- ⚡ **Philosophy**: `distribution > features`. Obsessed with getting products into users' hands.
- 🧪 **Hackathon Regular**: Built **ZeroVision** (an AI waste classifier) and constantly pushing boundaries in 24-hour sprints.
- 🎵 **AB Acoustics**: When I'm not coding, I'm making music. Check out my Hindi covers and originals on Instagram!
